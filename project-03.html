Yespark

Yespark is a French digital parking company that lets drivers rent parking spaces, mainly through a subscription model, and helps owners monetize underused parking spots. Its core business is the long-term rental and management of parking spaces for cars, motorbikes, and bikes, with a platform that simplifies access to private parking in cities.

Task
Rebuild the brand style guidelines book, produce static and motion graphic assets, adapt the TV advertising campaign into a short format for social media.
Role/Services
Brand Design
Client
Yespark
Year
2023
Live Site

Between 2019 and 2022, the company doubled its workforce and expanded the number of parking spaces it manages to 60,000. Since 2021, Yespark has also begun offering the installation of electric vehicle charging stations in selected parking spaces.

These developments called for an evolution of the brand’s guidelines, ensuring greater consistency across all touchpoints, from B2B and B2C communications to internal communications. It was equally important that these guidelines remain clear, intuitive, and easy to adopt for new employees and external collaborators.
